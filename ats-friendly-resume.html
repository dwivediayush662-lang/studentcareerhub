How to Create an ATS-Friendly Resume in 2026

Learn how to create a simple, professional and ATS-friendly resume that recruiters can easily read.

A resume is one of the most important documents when applying for a job or internship. For many companies, resumes are first processed by an Applicant Tracking System (ATS) before they are reviewed by a recruiter.

This is why students and freshers should create resumes that are simple, professional and easy for both ATS software and recruiters to understand.

Resume Tip: Keep your resume focused on the job you are applying for. Highlight relevant skills, projects, education and experience instead of adding unnecessary information.

1. Use a Simple Resume Format

An ATS-friendly resume should have a clean and simple layout. Avoid excessive graphics, complicated tables, multiple text columns and decorative elements that can make information difficult to parse.

Use clear headings such as Summary, Education, Skills, Projects, Experience and Certifications.

2. Add a Clear Professional Summary

Your professional summary should briefly explain who you are, your main skills and the type of opportunity you are looking for.

Example: Motivated computer science graduate with knowledge of Java, web development and problem solving. Interested in starting a career in software development and applying technical skills to real-world projects.

3. Add Relevant Skills

Create a dedicated skills section and list skills that are relevant to the job description.

Example:

Java
Python
HTML & CSS
JavaScript
SQL
Git & GitHub
Problem Solving

4. Include Projects

Projects are especially important for freshers because they demonstrate practical knowledge.

For every important project, mention the project name, technologies used and what you contributed.

Example:

Student Career Portal
Technologies: HTML, CSS, JavaScript
Created a responsive career website containing job resources, interview preparation and learning articles.

5. Add Education

List your most recent education first. Include your degree, college or university and relevant academic information.

Example:

Bachelor of Technology in Computer Science
University / College Name
2023 – 2027

6. Add Certifications

Certifications can demonstrate additional skills and learning, especially when you are a fresher.

Include certifications that are relevant to the position you are applying for.

7. Use Keywords From the Job Description

Read the job description carefully and identify important skills, technologies and qualifications.

If you genuinely have those skills, use the same terminology naturally in your resume.

For example, if a job description asks for Java, SQL and Git, make sure those skills are clearly mentioned in your resume when applicable.

8. Avoid Unnecessary Information

A resume should be concise and relevant. Avoid adding information that does not help demonstrate your suitability for the position.

For most freshers, a one-page resume is a good starting point if they can present their relevant qualifications clearly.

9. Check Your Resume for Errors

Spelling and grammar mistakes can make a resume look unprofessional.

Before applying, carefully check your resume for spelling, grammar, formatting and contact details.

10. Save Your Resume Correctly

Unless the employer specifies another format, PDF is commonly used when submitting a final resume.

Use a professional filename such as:

Ayush_Dwivedi_Resume.pdf

ATS-Friendly Resume Checklist

✔ Simple and clean layout
✔ Clear section headings
✔ Relevant keywords
✔ Job-related skills
✔ Projects and achievements
✔ Correct contact information
✔ No unnecessary graphics
✔ No spelling mistakes
✔ Consistent formatting
✔ Resume tailored to the job

Common Resume Mistakes Freshers Should Avoid

❌ Using too many colors and graphics
❌ Adding irrelevant skills
❌ Copying a generic resume for every job
❌ Writing very long paragraphs
❌ Including false information
❌ Making spelling mistakes
❌ Adding outdated or unnecessary details

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an ATS-friendly resume?

An ATS-friendly resume uses a clear structure, readable formatting and relevant information so Applicant Tracking Systems can more easily process the content.

Should a fresher resume be one page?

For many freshers, one page is enough to present relevant education, skills, projects and certifications clearly.

Should I use keywords in my resume?

Yes, when they accurately describe your skills or experience. Use relevant terminology from the job description naturally rather than adding keywords that do not apply to you.

Can freshers create an ATS-friendly resume?

Yes. Freshers can create an ATS-friendly resume by using a simple format and highlighting relevant education, technical skills, projects, certifications and achievements.
