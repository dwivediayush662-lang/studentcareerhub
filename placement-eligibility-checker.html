How Placement Eligibility Works

Companies may consider multiple factors before allowing students to participate in placement drives.

📈 CGPA

Some companies specify a minimum CGPA requirement for placement eligibility.

📚 Backlogs

Companies may restrict active backlogs during the selection process.

📝 Academic Percentage

10th and 12th percentage requirements may also be considered.

🎓 Education Gap

Certain recruitment drives may have restrictions related to education gaps.

Important: Placement eligibility criteria can change depending on the company, role, recruitment drive, college and year. Always verify the final eligibility criteria from the official company notification.
