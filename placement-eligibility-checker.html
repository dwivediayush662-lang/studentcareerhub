What is a Placement Eligibility Checker?

A placement eligibility checker is a tool that helps students understand whether their academic profile may match common company placement requirements. It can consider CGPA, 10th percentage, 12th percentage, active backlogs, cleared backlogs and education gaps.

Students can use this company eligibility checker before applying for campus placement drives to identify possible academic shortfalls and understand what they may need to improve.

Important: Placement eligibility criteria can change depending on the company, role, recruitment drive, college and year. The results provided by this tool are informational and should not be treated as an official recruitment decision. Always verify the final eligibility criteria from the official company notification.
